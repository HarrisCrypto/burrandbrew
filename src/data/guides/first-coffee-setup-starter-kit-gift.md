## Who this guide is for

Someone who drinks coffee every day, buys it pre-ground, and has never thought about why the café cup tastes better. This is the gift that answers that question — not with a lecture, but with a setup they can use on the first morning.

The rule that shapes the whole kit: **buy the grinder before you upgrade anything else.** Fresh grounds matter more than any brewer on this list. A beginner with a good grinder and a $9 dripper makes better coffee than a beginner with a fancy machine and stale pre-ground beans.

## What not to include

Do not add a blade grinder — it chops beans unevenly, and uneven grounds are the fastest way to a bitter cup. Do not add an espresso machine: espresso has a steep learning curve, needs grinder precision, and asks a beginner to change everything at once. And do not add a second brewer "just in case" — one method, learned well, beats two methods half-learned.
