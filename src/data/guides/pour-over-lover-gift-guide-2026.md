The icon. An hourglass of borosilicate glass with a wood collar, on permanent display at MoMA, brewing coffee since 1941. ![Illustration of a Kalita Wave dripper](/images/gift-guides/pour-over/kalita.png)

*Illustration — not a photo of the exact model.*

### Origami Dripper S Ceramic — $39

![Illustration of an Origami dripper](/images/gift-guides/pour-over/origami.png)

*Illustration — not a photo of the exact model.*

The beautiful one. A porcelain dripper from Japan's Mino pottery region, folded into twenty ridges that hold the filter off the walls so water drains freely. *Illustration — not a photo of the exact model.*

## What not to buy

Do not gift pre-ground supermarket coffee to a pour-over lover — the grind goes stale fast, and stale grounds undo everything a good dripper does. Do not buy filters without checking the size: V60 01 vs 02, Kalita 155 vs 185, and Chemex squares vs circles are not interchangeable, and the wrong box is a drawer filler. And do not gift a dripper without its filters or holder — a brewer that cannot brew on arrival is a chore, not a gift.
