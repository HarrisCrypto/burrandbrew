## The AI machine

### Mac mini — the dedicated home AI machine

The Local AI guide recommends a dedicated, always-on Mac mini as the home AI machine, on its own Apple ID with iCloud off, so your AI experiments live apart from your everyday computer. The sweet spot is a new Mac mini, M4 generation, with 16 GB of memory — it launched at $599 in the US. The budget route is a used Apple Silicon mini (M1 or M2) with 16 GB, often a few hundred dollars used, which runs the same starter models well.

Two things that never change: **memory cannot be upgraded after purchase on Apple Silicon Macs**, so if you are torn between sizes, memory is the upgrade that matters; and **prices move** — the M4 mini's prices have already moved since launch, so check the current listing before you buy.
