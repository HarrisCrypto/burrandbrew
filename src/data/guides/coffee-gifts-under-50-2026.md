## Who this guide is for

Someone who likes coffee but has not gone down the gear rabbit hole — or someone who already brews manually and would appreciate one good upgrade. Every pick below costs under $50, and most of them cost under $40. No espresso machine, no grinder that needs a second mortgage.

## What not to buy

Do not gift a blade grinder — it chops beans unevenly, and uneven grounds are the fastest way to a bitter cup. Do not gift a moka pot to someone with an induction stove unless you also buy the adapter plate. And do not gift a brewer without checking what they already own: a second V60 for someone who already has one is a drawer filler, not a gift.
