## The one rule

Black Friday is November 27, 2026, and the coffee aisle is full of crossed-out prices that were never real. The rule that saves you from all of them: **a deal is only a deal against a price you can verify.** Check the maker's own site, or the price the item actually sold at last month — not the number with a line through it. If you cannot verify the "before" price, it is not a discount, it is decoration.

## Skip: the Black Friday traps

- **Blade grinders.** They chop beans unevenly, and uneven grounds are the fastest way to a bitter cup — at any price.
- **Mystery-brand espresso machines.** A $99 machine with no brand, no parts support, and no reviews is not a bargain; it is a return in a box. If they want espresso, the honest entry point is a real machine and a real grinder, and the grinder matters more.
- **"Deal" bundles stuffed with filler.** A brewer bundled with a dozen accessories you did not ask for is a bundle of things nobody wanted. Buy the one good thing instead.
- **A brewer without checking what they already own.** A second V60 for someone who already has one is a drawer filler, not a gift.

## Before you click

1. **Check the size.** Tampers and portafilters must match the machine's basket size — 58mm for most E61-style machines, 53.3mm for Breville/Sage. Moka pot "cups" are 2 oz; French press "cups" are 4 oz.
2. **Check the listing, not the ad.** Prices move, especially in the holidays. The number in the guide is the anchor; the listing is the truth.
3. **Order the big things early.** Large items — espresso machines, grinders — sell out and ship slower in December. If the gift is a splurge, do not leave it to the last week.
