# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

Burr & Brew ("we", "this site") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why, in plain language.

## What we collect

- **Anonymous analytics:** we may use privacy-friendly analytics to see which articles are read. No cross-site tracking, no data sold.
- **Nothing else.** This site has no accounts, no comments, and no newsletter — there is nothing to sign up for and no personal data to hand over.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect email addresses or personal data.
- We don't sell personal data. Ever.
- We don't run third-party ad trackers.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. When you click one, the merchant may set a cookie to credit us for the referral — that's between you and the merchant, governed by their privacy policy. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
